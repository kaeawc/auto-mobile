ProtoViewer is a viewer for the design prototypes you author with AutoMobile. Keep every prototype in one library, open any of them in a tap, and carry them with you without a network connection.

What you can do

- Browse a library of prototypes with their names and when you last opened them.
- Switch between prototypes in one tap, relaunch one from the start, or reset it to clear its saved values.
- Import a prototype from a file, or open one shared with you.
- Share a prototype as a single .automobile-proto bundle through the system share sheet.
- Review a prototype exactly as the spec describes it: layout, motion, theme, saved values and data, rendered on your device.

Works on its own

The library starts empty. Import a .automobile-proto file from Files or the share sheet and it opens right away, with no other app, account or connection on your phone. Prototypes are declarative documents, not programs: the renderer's features are fixed in the app, and a prototype cannot install or run code.

Private by default

ProtoViewer needs no account and shows no ads. Prototypes stay on your device. Network access for imported prototypes is off until you turn it on in the library settings.

Made for designers and developers

Author a prototype with the AutoMobile tools, save it to your library, and review it on a real phone. ProtoViewer is open source under the Apache License 2.0.
